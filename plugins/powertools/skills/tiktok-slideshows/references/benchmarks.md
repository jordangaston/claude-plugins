# Benchmarks and Diagnostics

Practitioner benchmarks for TikTok slideshows. These are directional targets from creator-economy sources, not official TikTok figures — always defer to the user's own TikTok Studio analytics. TikTok has not published carousel ranking weights.

## How the algorithm treats carousels

- Each post gets a small-batch test (~200–500 viewers); strong signals earn wider distribution. The first few hours matter most.

- Ranking signals, roughly in order of weight: **saves**, comments, swipe-through rate, completion rate, dwell time per slide, reverse swipes (a strong positive), shares, likes.

- Carousels also surface in TikTok search because slide text is OCR'd — keyword-rich slides rank.

## Target metrics

| Metric | Healthy | Exceptional |

|---|---|---|

| Swipe-through off slide 1 | >60% | — |

| Completion (8+ slides) | 40–60% | — |

| Save rate | >3% | >5% |

| Comment rate | >0.5% of views | — |

| Swipe-through (e-commerce benchmark) | >20% | — |

## Symptom → diagnosis → fix

| Symptom | Diagnosis | Fix |

|---|---|---|

| Low swipe-through off slide 1 | Weak hook | Rewrite 5 hooks, re-test the top 2 on different days |

| Slide 1 retains but completion collapses mid-deck | Weak/long build slides | Cut filler, strengthen slide 2, shorten the deck |

| Views fine, saves low | Not reference-worthy | Add checklists, frameworks, "save this" value |

| Need cold-audience reach, not saves | Wrong format for goal | Mix in short video — it out-shares carousels by ~33% |

| Unoriginal-content flag | Recycled/template content | Stop delete-and-repost; add original copy, images, framing per post |

| 80% see slide 1, 30% reach slide 5 | Slides 2–3 leak | Revise slides 2–3 specifically |

## Engagement context

- TikTok's own creator messaging (Feb 2024): carousels average 1.9× more likes, 2.9× more comments, 2.6× more shares than video. Self-interested source.

- Independent (Fanpage Karma, 698K posts, Jan–May 2025): 81% higher engagement, 82% more likes, ~33% fewer shares than video. Design for saves/comments, not viral spread.

- Cadence (Buffer, 11.4M posts, Oct 2025): moving from 1 post/week to 2–5 yields up to 17% more views per post; 6–10 posts → 29%; 11+ → 34%.

## Monetization notes

- Slideshows are generally ineligible for Creator Rewards (requires 1-minute+ video).

- Revenue paths: TikTok Shop affiliate (~1,000 followers), sponsorships (outreach ~5,000), bio-link funnels (day one).

- TikTok tightened unoriginal-content enforcement Sept 15, 2025; repeated flags can revoke monetization. Verify current policy before building a monetization plan.
