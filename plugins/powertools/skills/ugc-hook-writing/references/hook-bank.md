# The UGC Hook Bank & Templates

Steal these. Each hook is a named pattern with a fill-in example. Mix the angle to the product and write at least three per test, from three different angles.

**Rule: never lead with the product.** The product is Beat 3, never Beat 1. Lead with the human truth, the problem, or the curiosity. Then the product enters as the relief.

## The 32-pattern Hook Bank

### Problem-callout hooks
1. **The money-burned hook:** "I spent £200 on [category] this year. This £28 one does more than all of it."
2. **The wasted-years hook:** "I wasted three years doing [thing] the hard way before I found this."
3. **The frustration callout:** "If you're still [annoying old way], stop. There's a better way and nobody told me either."
4. **The you're-not-crazy hook:** "It's not just you. [Common product] genuinely doesn't work, and here's what does."
5. **The expensive-mistake hook:** "I almost dropped £200 on a professional [product] until I found this £20 one."

### Curiosity / open-loop hooks
6. **The wait-for-it hook:** "Wait. Watch what this does to my [skin/hair/whatever] in real time."
7. **The unexplained-result hook:** "I don't fully understand why this works, but look."
8. **The I-have-to-show-you hook:** "Okay I wasn't going to post this but I have to show someone."
9. **The cliffhanger:** "I changed ONE thing in my routine and the difference is kind of insane."
10. **The nobody's-talking-about hook:** "Why is nobody talking about this?"

### Social-proof hooks
11. **The everyone's-asking hook:** "Everyone keeps asking what I'm using, so here it is."
12. **The sold-out hook:** "This sold out three times and I finally get why."
13. **The my-friend-made-me hook:** "My friend wouldn't shut up about this so I caved. She was right."
14. **The number hook:** "[Big number] people switched to this. Here's why."

### Negative / contrarian hooks
15. **The stop-doing-X hook:** "Stop doing [common thing] immediately. You're wasting money and nobody's telling you."
16. **The unpopular opinion:** "Unpopular opinion: you don't need a 10-step routine. You need this one thing."
17. **The I-was-wrong hook:** "I used to think [product category] was a scam. I was wrong."
18. **The honest-flaw hook:** "I'll be honest, this has one annoying flaw. But I still use it every day, here's why."
19. **The don't-buy-this-if hook:** "Don't buy this if you [X]. But if you're like me, keep watching."

### Controversy / insider hooks
20. **The industry-secret hook:** "The [industry] doesn't want you to know about this ingredient."
21. **The they-hate-this hook:** "[Experts] are going to hate me for posting this."
22. **The overcharged hook:** "You're being massively overcharged for basic ingredients. Let me show you."
23. **The whisper hook:** *(leans in, lowers voice)* "Okay so nobody's supposed to know this…"

### Demo / visual-action hooks
24. **The satisfying-texture hook:** *(no talking, just applying product)* visual ASMR that stops the zombie scroll.
25. **The before-and-after hook:** "This was my [thing] three days ago. This is now."
26. **The real-time hook:** "I'm going to use this on camera right now so you can see it's not edited."
27. **The unboxing hook:** "Came in the post today, opening it on camera so you see exactly what you get."

### Relatable / story hooks
28. **The POV hook:** "POV: you finally found the [product] that actually works."
29. **The confession hook:** "I genuinely didn't think this would work and now I'm obsessed." *(Skeptic-to-believer, the testimonial engine.)*
30. **The girl-who hook:** "Be the girl who [aspirational outcome] instead of [current pain]."
31. **The mid-routine hook:** "So I'm just getting ready and I have to tell you about this." *(Feels caught mid-life, like a real makeup-routine clip.)*
32. **The this-is-your-sign hook:** "This is your sign to finally try [product]."

## The 5-beat script template

```
PRODUCT: _____________________
CORE PROMISE (one sentence): _____________________
HOOK ANGLE: [ problem-callout / curiosity / social-proof / negative / controversy / demo / story ]

BEAT 1, HOOK (0 to 3s)
   Line: "_____________________"
   (Pattern-interrupt. No product. Earn the next second.)

BEAT 2, AGITATE (3 to 8s)
   Line: "_____________________"
   (Name the real frustration. Make them feel seen.)

BEAT 3, PRODUCT (8 to 15s)
   Line: "_____________________"
   (The turn. "Then I found this." Casual, gatekeeper energy.)

BEAT 4, PROOF / DEMO (15 to 25s)
   Line: "_____________________"
   Action: _____________________ (show the result / product in hand)
   (Specific + understated. Numbers and concrete detail.)

BEAT 5, CTA (25 to 30s)
   Line: "_____________________"
   (One soft next step. "Link's in my bio.")
```

## Skeptical-testimonial line bank

Drop-in lines for the skeptic-to-believer arc.

**Opening doubt:**
- "I genuinely didn't think this would work."
- "I'll be honest, I assumed this was overhyped."
- "I'm the most skeptical person alive when it comes to this stuff."
- "I almost didn't buy it because of the price."

**The failed past:**
- "I'd tried literally everything before this."
- "I'd already wasted so much money on stuff that did nothing."
- "Nothing I used actually fixed it."

**The turn:**
- "Then within [timeframe], I actually noticed a difference."
- "And I'm not exaggerating, the change was kind of ridiculous."
- "I did a double take, honestly."

**The reluctant recommendation:**
- "I don't usually post things like this, but I had to."
- "I'm not getting paid to say this, I just genuinely use it."
- "Just try it once and you'll get it."

## Worked example: one product, 3 hooks + a full 30s script

**Product:** "GlowDrop", a £24 vitamin C serum.
**Core promise:** Visibly brighter, calmer skin in under a week, for a fraction of the price of premium brands.

### Step 1: Three hooks, three angles (for the test)

- **Hook A — Problem-callout:** "I spent over £200 on serums this year. This £24 one quietly outperformed every single one of them."
- **Hook B — Controversy/insider:** "Skincare brands genuinely don't want you to know this, but you're paying for the packaging, not the ingredients."
- **Hook C — Skeptic/story:** "I did NOT think a £24 serum was going to do anything. I owe it an apology."

Generate all three, run them, scale whichever pulls the best cost-per-result. Below is the full script built on Hook A.

### Step 2: Full 30-second testimonial script

```
BEAT 1, HOOK (0 to 3s) (getting ready, casual, half-laughing)
"Okay I spent over £200 on serums this year... and this £24 one quietly beat all of them."

BEAT 2, AGITATE (3 to 8s)
"I'd basically given up. Everything I tried either did nothing or made my skin angry, and I was so tired of throwing money at it."

BEAT 3, PRODUCT (8 to 15s) (holds up the bottle to camera)
"Then my sister made me try this. GlowDrop. I genuinely expected nothing."

BEAT 4, PROOF / DEMO (15 to 25s) (applies a few drops, leans toward camera)
"Four days in and the redness around my nose is just... gone. My skin actually looks awake. No filter, this is just it."

BEAT 5, CTA (25 to 30s)
"I'm not getting paid to say this, I just had to. Link's in my bio if you want to try it. Twenty-four quid, honestly just do it."
```

**Why it works:** skeptic-to-believer arc, contractions everywhere, one specific result ("redness around my nose, four days"), product enters at Beat 3 not Beat 1, soft CTA, and every beat fits inside a ~7-second clip so it maps straight onto VEO/Sora batching.

## Common mistakes (the 9 ways UGC dies)

1. **Leading with the product.** "Introducing GlowDrop, the serum that…" Dead on arrival. Product is Beat 3.
2. **Writing brand-speak.** "Revolutionary, dermatologist-approved, game-changing." No human talks like that.
3. **One hook only.** You're not clever enough to guess the winner, and neither is anyone. Write three, test, scale.
4. **Cramming too much into one breath.** If the creator can't say the line naturally in 7 seconds, the AI rushes it and glitches. Cut it down.
5. **No skepticism in a testimonial.** A person thrilled from word one isn't believable. Start doubtful.
6. **Vague proof.** "It works amazing" sells nothing. "Four days, redness gone" sells. Get specific.
7. **A hard CTA.** "BUY NOW LIMITED TIME" breaks the casual, peer-to-peer spell. Keep it soft.
8. **Too polished.** Realism comes from imperfection now. A little stumble beats a flawless robotic read.
9. **Ignoring the data.** You wrote it, it bombed, you loved it anyway. Doesn't matter — ad performance decides, not taste.
